HomenS.AI JobPilot

A local AI agent finds companies and vacancies near you, prepares a tailored CV and cover letter for each one, tracks every reply and builds a monthly report. All working data is kept in one folder on your computer.

Idea and development: Serhii KhomenkoCodex or Claude CodeJSON files, no server

Current stage: experimental documentation and agent workflow. There is no separate job-search or mail-sending application. The statistics view is the exception: its HTML/CSS/JavaScript validates, sorts, filters and displays a snapshot written by the agent; it does not search or send mail. Full agent/Gmail acceptance is not complete. What the package contains.

01 · Workflow

The journey of one application

The agent runs the workflow in a chat. Everything that must be reliable is saved in the project folder, not in the chat.

  1. Your storyYour documents and your own words, by voice or in text, become a knowledge base. You confirm every fact.
  2. SearchCompanies and vacancies within the chosen radius. Nearby places come first; journeys are checked.
  3. MatchingVacancy requirements are compared with the confirmed experience.
  4. DocumentsA tailored CV, cover letter and email text, checked visually as PDF.
  5. ScheduleImmediately, every working day or every second day, with holidays and the monthly limit respected.
  6. GmailAn authorized connection, a check of recipient and attachments, whole threads read before any reply.
  7. TrackingReplies, delivery failures and proof of sending, without duplicates.
  8. ReportA neutral summary in the chosen language and a separate page of market recommendations.

The user chooses the radius and the means of travel (car or public transport). Journey time, transfers, walking distance and the evening return are all assessed. Adding a town does not change the user's priorities: the search area grows step by step.

03 · Principles

Rules that do not change

No more than 44 per month

At most 44 CV submissions per person per calendar month, counted across all profiles and chats. The 45th is blocked before it is sent.

Drafts first

A new setup only prepares drafts. Sending is switched on separately for each profile and each Gmail account.

Only confirmed facts

The agent never invents experience, dates, certificates or language levels. The user confirms each fact.

Statistics are view-only

The HTML page shows a snapshot. The agent updates the data; the page cannot change records or read Gmail.

Mail stays untouched

No message is deleted, archived or relabelled.

No secrets in the folder

Passwords and tokens are never stored in project files or published on GitHub.
