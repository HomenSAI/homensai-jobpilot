# Version history

Short summary of every release in plain words. Technical details are in CHANGELOG.md. One line per release: `- version | date | text`.

- 1.19.0 | 10 October 2026 | Statistics page: a new block estimates what the saved time is worth, at the German minimum wage, per month and per year (rough estimate, with sources).
- 1.18.4 | 10 October 2026 | Statistics page: the monthly-limit bar now shows used, reserved and left right on the bar.
- 1.18.3 | 10 October 2026 | Time saved is recalculated: one application takes 30 minutes in all.
- 1.18.2 | 10 October 2026 | Statistics page: the monthly-limit bars are smaller and the text no longer runs out of the card.
- 1.18.1 | 10 October 2026 | Safety page: the author's name now links to his website.
- 1.18.0 | 10 October 2026 | Tried the first setup on three made-up people and corrected the rules where the instructions were unclear (for example what to do when a fact is not confirmed). Fixed the version number shown in the corner of the pages, which had stayed old.
- 1.17.1 | 10 October 2026 | Fixed two inconsistencies in the sending rules and added an automatic check that stops a release if personal data or secrets would be published. Added example output of a simulated run on made-up data.
- 1.17.0 | 10 October 2026 | Tidied the Roadmap page: the version history is now written in plain language, and the list of remaining developer work moved to a separate file on GitHub. The Author page lost its experience and projects sections and got a warmer introduction.
- 1.16.3 | 10 October 2026 | Removed an outdated note on the Safety page.
- 1.16.2 | 10 October 2026 | Safety page: added a clear statement that the AI agent you choose, and its provider, are responsible for where your data goes once the agent has read it.
- 1.16.1 | 10 October 2026 | Removed a confusing technical diagram from the Architecture page.
- 1.16.0 | 10 October 2026 | Employer replies are now saved in full in the folder of each application: who wrote, when, the full text and any attachments. Two diagrams on the Architecture page were rewritten in plain words.
- 1.15.1 | 10 October 2026 | The section on CV and cover-letter templates was redesigned so that it is tidier and easier to follow.
- 1.15.0 | 10 October 2026 | Added a start page, index.html, that links to every page and document in all four languages.
- 1.14.5 | 10 October 2026 | Every page now shows the version number in the top-left corner, so you can tell which version you downloaded.
- 1.14.4 | 10 October 2026 | Added a clear explanation of what to put into the CV and cover-letter template folders.
- 1.14.3 | 10 October 2026 | Fixed the language buttons, which were squeezed or cut off on narrow screens.
- 1.14.2 | 10 October 2026 | Found gaps and differences between the four language editions and removed them.
- 1.14.1 | 10 October 2026 | Corrected confusing wording about the template folders.
- 1.14.0 | 10 October 2026 | Added Ukrainian as the fourth language, with neutral CV, cover-letter and report templates. The translation still has to be checked by a native speaker.
- 1.13.0 | 10 October 2026 | Added the photo rule: a photo goes into a CV only if you choose it, and never into a cover letter. Removed a page that compared other projects and did not belong here.
- 1.12.0 | 10 October 2026 | Added the Author page, moved the links to the official tool documentation to the Safety page, and corrected a few inaccurate statements.
- 1.11.0 | 10 October 2026 | Added the site icon and interactive diagrams on the Architecture page.
- 1.10.0 | 10 October 2026 | Clarified the limit: only mail sent through JobPilot counts towards the 44 a month, not your own mail. Two applications in a row to the same address are now blocked. Choosing CV and letter templates was completed.
- 1.9.0 | 10 October 2026 | The record of actions is now kept month by month, so it stays manageable.
- 1.8.0 | 10 October 2026 | You can now choose how many applications may go out per day (1 to 43). The monthly limit of 44 stays.
- 1.7.0 | 10 October 2026 | Large safety review: stricter checks before any e-mail is sent, protection against sending twice, a German edition and a more reliable statistics page. Not yet tested with real Gmail.
- 1.6.0 | 9 October 2026 | Every application now has its own tidy folder. Statuses became clearer (waiting, invited, rejected and why), the statistics page shows shares and time saved, and version numbers are now assigned automatically.
- 1.5.x | 9 October 2026 | Added monthly planning, a daily mail check, scheduled sending through Gmail with a manual alternative, a readiness page and the limit of 44 a month.
- 1.4.x | 7–8 October 2026 | The agent can now start by itself: instruction files, ready folders for your materials, and a first setup that begins with your own story. An interrupted setup now resumes where it stopped.
- 1.3.x | 7 October 2026 | Made clear that JobPilot is rules and files only, with no program code. Statistics data moved into its own file.
- 1.2.0 | 7 October 2026 | First release: overview pages, installation guide and statistics page.
- 1.0–1.1 | before 7 October 2026 | Earlier drafts before the project was published.
