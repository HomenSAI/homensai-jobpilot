Roadmap

What exists, what is next

This page separates what is finished from what is only planned, so that nothing planned is mistaken for something that already works.

01 · Today

What exists

Finished

  • Rules for the AI agent in four languages: English, Russian, German and Ukrainian.
  • Instruction files for the agent and ready folders for your materials.
  • An illustrated overview and a step-by-step installation guide.
  • The statistics page: your applications, replies, shares and time saved, with sorting and filters.
  • Monthly planning, a daily mail check, scheduled sending through Gmail with a manual alternative, local backups and reports.
  • Employer replies are saved in full in each application’s folder.
  • Neutral templates for the CV, cover letter and report, in Word and PDF.
  • A simulated run of the rules on made-up data (five applications with replies, errors and limits) and a dry run of the first setup on three made-up people.
  • An automatic check that stops a release if personal data or secrets would be published.

Not done yet

  • Trying the agent with real people: someone who has prepared nothing, someone who has filled the folders, and a voice interview (only a trial on made-up people has been done).
  • Connecting Gmail and checking the exact setup.
  • A full trial by a real user: agent, Gmail, documents and schedule.
  • Checking the English, German and Ukrainian texts with native speakers.
  • Checking the documents in Microsoft Word.
  • Neutral CV, cover-letter and report templates in German and Russian.
  • Using real vacancy and route sources.
  • A rights check of all included materials before a release.
