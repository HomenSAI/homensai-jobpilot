Profile

A master profile: more than a CV

Put an existing CV and supporting material into the profile folder, or fill in the questionnaire. The agent shows each extracted fact with its source, and you confirm it. The full biography stays in the project; every application receives only the relevant part.

01 · Contents

What the profile records

Experience

Employers, roles, dates, tasks, tools, teams and results.

Education

School, vocational training, university, courses, recognition of qualifications, diplomas and certificates.

Skills

Languages, digital and technical skills, level and examples.

Projects

Internships, volunteering, portfolio, informal learning, achievements.

Preferences

Target roles, working arrangements, radius, transport, journey time, schedule.

Languages and reports

Separate language settings for the CV, cover letter, email and report; an optional report template.

Sources

For every fact: a document or a self-description, a date, the verification status and permission to use it.
02 · Questionnaire

From a broad profile to a short CV

The profile holds a wide record of confirmed skills and experience. For each application the agent writes a concise, readable CV in the language you selected.

What the profile holds

Education and training, experience in each role, languages, digital and professional skills, projects, achievements, volunteering, certificates and source documents. It also records measurable results, tools, STAR examples, target roles and mobility.

What the employer receives

Only selected, verified facts that matter for the vacancy. The full biography stays local. Sensitive data and a photograph are not collected unless you add them yourself.

03 · Report

A neutral report in the chosen language

If you supply a report template, the agent works on a copy and keeps the original unchanged. Without a template it creates a table with surname, first name, period, submission date, company, vacancy, channel, reply date and a short outcome. No institutional fields or third-party branding are added. A missing reply is labelled in the report language; an actual reply is summarized by its reason or decision, not by whether a thank-you was sent.
