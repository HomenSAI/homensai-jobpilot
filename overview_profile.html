Profile

Your life story in one file

Put your documents into five ready folders, or simply tell the agent about yourself by voice. The agent writes your whole life story as facts into one file, the knowledge base, and you confirm every fact. Each application then takes only the relevant, confirmed part.

Folders are ready in the ZIPVoice or textFacts need your confirmation
01 · Folders

Where to put your materials

The only place where you have to put anything by hand is Локальные_данные\Положите_сюда (“put here”). It already exists in the project folder. The agent only reads it and never changes your files.

FolderWhat to put there
1_Мои_документы
my documents		CVs of any version, diplomas, certificates, references, official letters, a portfolio, samples of your letters. Any format, no sorting.
2_Рассказ_о_себе
my story		Your story in your own words: a transcript of a voice recording, notes, answers to the prepared questions. The agent also saves interviews here, word for word.
3_Шаблон_отчёта
report template		Only if you have your own report sample.
4_Шаблон_резюме
CV layout		Your CV sample in the layout you want for submissions, if you have one. DOCX or PDF.
5_Шаблон_сопроводительного_письма
cover letter layout		Your cover letter in your own design, if you have one. DOCX or PDF.

You can fill the folders before the first run or leave them empty. How to prepare them, including the voice recording: Install → Prepare your materials.

02 · Interview

Tell it by voice or in text

If you have nothing prepared, the agent interviews you. It asks one topic at a time, accepts long spoken answers, saves them word for word after every topic, and says back what it understood.

Who you are

Name, place, languages, what you want, what you do not want.

Every job

Employer, place, role, period, daily tasks, tools, team, results.

Education and certificates

School, training, university, courses, who issued what and when.

Languages and skills

Level of reading, writing, speaking; technical and computer skills, where and when used.

Projects and real situations

Internships, volunteering, projects; three to five stories: the task, what you did, the result.

Conditions and privacy

Radius, transport, schedule, start date; what must never be shown to an employer.

The agent does not ask about health, religion or family status. If you mention such things yourself, they are marked as sensitive and never go into a CV without your explicit choice.

03 · Knowledge base

One file with your whole life story

From your documents and your story the agent writes the file Локальные_данные\persons\<your id>\База_знаний\Жизнеописание.md. It is written in your language, you can open and read it in any text editor, and you can correct it by hand.

How a fact looks

- [F012] Mechanical engineer at Muster GmbH, Stuttgart, 2012–2016; commissioning of packaging lines | source: Zeugnis_Muster.pdf p.2 | status: confirmed 2026-10-07 | use: cv | sens: normal

One fact per line, with its source, its status (proposed, confirmed or rejected), whether it may appear in application documents (use) and whether it is sensitive (sens).

Rules

  • The agent first writes every fact as proposed; only your confirmation makes it confirmed.
  • A fact is never deleted: a correction adds a new line and marks the old one rejected.
  • A fact without a document stays valid, marked self-reported.
  • CVs and letters use only confirmed facts that are allowed for documents.
  • Your own words are kept unchanged in 2_Рассказ_о_себе.
04 · The search

What the agent asks about the search

After your story and documents, the agent asks what we are looking for and how. One question at a time; the answers are saved as profile.json.

The agent asksYou choose
What are we looking for?Job, Ausbildung, internship (Praktikum), Probearbeit, temporary work — any combination; which roles and industries; what to exclude; employment type; the earliest start date
Where do you live?The city, and if you wish the district or street. It is used only to plan routes
How far?The search radius in kilometres
How do you get there?Car · train · bus or tram (public transport) · bicycle · on foot — any combination
How demanding is the way?The longest journey in minutes, the number of transfers, the walk, whether the evening return must be possible
Which towns first?Preferred towns in order of priority
Which languages?The CV, the cover letter, the email text and the report — five separate answers, each in one of four languages
How to send?At once · every working day · every second working day; the time of day. Nothing is sent without your permission

Everything else keeps safe defaults: the limit of 44 submissions per month, drafts only, subject up to 50 characters, a market page in the report.

05 · From the file to a CV

A short CV for every vacancy

The knowledge base holds the broad record. For each application the agent writes a concise, readable CV and cover letter in the language you selected, from confirmed facts only, and shows which fact each statement comes from. You see the gaps and decide what to clarify. A photo is not requested. It is included only if you choose it explicitly.

A neutral report in the chosen language. If you put a report template into 3_Шаблон_отчёта, the agent works on a copy and keeps the original unchanged. Without a template it creates a table with surname, first name, period, submission date, company, vacancy, channel, reply date and a short outcome. No institutional fields or third-party branding are added. A missing reply is labelled in the report language; an actual reply is summarized by its reason or decision, not by whether a thank-you was sent.
