01 · Privacy

Privacy and confirmed facts

Separate profiles

Each person has their own documents, account and journal. Search profiles of one person share the 44-submission limit; data of different people is kept apart.

Traceable facts

Every fact is linked to a document or a confirmed self-description. The AI must not invent dates, achievements, skills, certificates or language levels.

Authorized access

OAuth covers only the chosen account and the required actions. Secrets are held by the connection mechanism, outside the publishable project; every action is checked against the profile's permissions.

“Stored locally” does not mean “processed offline”. Information that a cloud agent reads may reach its provider, so consent is obtained before the agent first reads personal originals.

02 · Terms

An experimental product

Research and interpretation

Searching for companies and vacancies, extracting requirements, summaries and proposed reply classifications are done by the external agent you choose (for example ChatGPT/Codex or Anthropic/Claude) using the sources it finds. JobPilot defines the workflow, keeps the records and helps prepare documents and track progress. It does not guarantee that the whole market is covered or that every fact is read correctly.

Checking the results

Availability of vacancies, current contact details, routes and employer replies are not guaranteed. Before deciding or sending, check the employer, the requirements, the dates, the documents and the recipient. The product is used at your own risk; you remain responsible for your decisions and the information you share.
